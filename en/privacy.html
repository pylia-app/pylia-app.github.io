Article 1. Definitions

  • "Application": the Pylia Sleep mobile application, available on Apple's App Store.
  • "Publisher": PYLIA, which publishes the Application.
  • "User" or "you": any person who uses the Application.
  • "Personal data": any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, within the meaning of Article 4 of the GDPR.

Article 2. Data controller

The data controller is PYLIA. For any question about your personal data, you can write to: pylia.app+sleep@gmail.com.

Article 3. Principle: local processing, no account

The Application works without an account and without any server owned by the Publisher. The data you enter or that the Application calculates is stored only on your device. The Publisher never has access to it and never receives it.

Article 4. Data processed

4.1 Data stored on your device

  • Sleep data: bedtimes and wake-up times, length of nights and naps, number of snoozes.
  • Answers to the wake-up check-in: sleep quality, energy, mood, soreness, planned workout.
  • Calculated data: recovery score, advice, badges and statistics.
  • Preferences: alarm sound, language, appearance and other settings.
  • Optional profile: the first name or nickname and the avatar you choose.
  • Evening "brain dump" notes, if you use this feature.
  • Sleep diary and program: time to fall asleep, night awakenings, current week, lessons read and actions checked.
  • Apple Health data, only if you allow it: sleep stages, heart rate, heart rate variability, respiratory rate, oxygen saturation, wrist temperature, breathing disturbances, steps, exercise, time in daylight, caffeine, alcohol and mindfulness sessions from the last 30 days, read-only.
  • Weekly check-ins (severity score, sleepiness, mood), safety questionnaire answers and program exercises.

Answers about your sleep and how you feel may constitute data concerning health within the meaning of Article 9 of the GDPR. They never leave your device.

Apple Health data is read on your device, never transmitted and never used for advertising. Questions asked to the coach are processed by Apple's artificial intelligence model running on your device: the Publisher neither receives nor stores them.

The PDF report is created on your device and is only shared if you choose to send it.

The "My progress" story is an image created on your device from your figures (hours of sleep, score, time to fall asleep, efficiency, avatar). It is only shared if you choose to send it or save it to your photos; once posted on a social network, its distribution is governed by that network's terms.

4.2 Data not collected

Apart from the items above, which stay on your device, the Application does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, advertising identifier, bank details or any usage statistics.

Article 6. Recipients

No data is sold, rented or transferred. The only possible recipients are:

  • Apple, if you have enabled device backup (iCloud), under the terms of its own privacy policy.
  • Apple, as the App Store payment processor, if you subscribe to Pylia Premium. Payment is handled entirely by Apple: the Publisher never has access to your bank details.

Article 7. Transfers outside the European Union

The Publisher does not transfer any data outside the European Union. Data handled by Apple (iCloud backup, payments) is processed by Apple in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Article 8. Retention

Your data stays on your device as long as the Application is installed. You can delete it at any time from Settings → Delete history. Uninstalling the Application deletes all of its data.

Article 9. Security

Data is stored in the Application's own space on your device and benefits from the sandboxing and encryption provided by iOS. The Application does not send any of your data over the Internet.

Article 10. Device permissions

The Application may ask for the following permissions, all optional and changeable at any time in iPhone Settings:

  • Alarms: ring even in silent or Do Not Disturb mode;
  • Apple Health: read the nights recorded by a watch, a ring or another app (read-only);
  • Notifications: remind you to answer the check-in.
  • Notifications: remind you in the evening to start your bedtime routine.

Article 11. Cookies and trackers

The Application uses no cookies, no advertising trackers and no analytics tools.

Article 12. Your rights

Under Articles 15 to 22 of the GDPR, you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, as well as the right to withdraw your consent at any time.

Since your data is kept on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly (viewing it in the Application, deleting it from Settings, removing permissions in iOS). For any request: pylia.app+sleep@gmail.com.

You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL: www.cnil.fr).

Article 13. Minors

Users under the age of digital consent in their country (15 in France) must obtain a parent's permission to use the Application.

Article 14. Changes to this policy

The Publisher may change this policy, in particular if the Application or the regulations change. Any new version is shown in the Application and must be accepted before continuing to use it.

Article 15. Contact

For any question about this policy: pylia.app+sleep@gmail.com.
