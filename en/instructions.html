Article 1. Intended use

Pylia is a wellness app for adults who want to improve their sleep. It offers a 6-week program inspired by behavioral methods recognized by sleep research: sleep education, sleep diary, sleep window, stimulus control, calming thoughts, rhythm and environment, relapse prevention.

Pylia is not a CE-marked medical device. It does not diagnose, does not replace a consultation and does not offer any medication.

Article 2. Intended users

  • Adults aged 18 and over.
  • People who struggle to fall asleep or stay asleep, or who wake up tired.
  • People able to follow a daily program of a few minutes on their own.

Article 3. Contraindications and precautions

Reducing time in bed (sleep window) is not recommended without medical advice in case of:

  • epilepsy;
  • bipolar disorder;
  • pregnancy;
  • marked daytime sleepiness or unintended dozing off;
  • professional driving or operating dangerous machinery;
  • age under 18.

The app's safety questionnaire detects these situations: the sleep window then stays gentle (never under 7 h). Don't drive if you feel sleepy.

Article 4. When to see a doctor

  • Loud snoring, breathing pauses during sleep or low night-time oxygen.
  • An irresistible urge to move your legs in the evening.
  • Marked daytime sleepiness.
  • Low mood or dark thoughts: call your local emergency number (112 in Europe).
  • Sleep difficulties that persist after the program.
  • Night or shift work.

Article 5. Expected effects and side effects

The first weeks of the sleep window often come with temporary tiredness or sleepiness. Pylia monitors it in each weekly check-in and automatically widens the window by 30 minutes if it becomes marked.

Results vary from person to person and are not guaranteed.

Article 6. Using Pylia well

  • Fill in your diary every morning, even after a bad night.
  • Read the lesson and do the actions of each week.
  • Answer each weekly check-in.
  • Stick to your sleep window and wake-up time.
  • If needed, export the PDF report for your coach or your doctor.

Article 7. Data reliability

Diary durations are estimated from your answers. Data read from Apple Health depends on the device that measures it (watch, ring, iPhone). No value shown has diagnostic value.

Article 8. Report a problem

If you notice a side effect, a calculation error or abnormal behavior, write to pylia.app+sleep@gmail.com and mention the app version (Settings → Version).
